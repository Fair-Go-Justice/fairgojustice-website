We believe petition integrity matters as much as petition numbers. Here is our roadmap to a fully verified, government-grade signature system — and exactly where we stand today.

Current Status (July 2026): We are actively transitioning from email-engagement tracking to a verified digital identity system. Current supporter count: 12,847. These are real people who engaged with this campaign — we are now building the infrastructure to formally verify and count each one.

WHY INTEGRITY MATTERS

Any movement calling for accountability in the justice system must hold itself to the same standard. That means being transparent about what our numbers represent — and building towards a system where every signature is cryptographically verified to a real Australian identity.

We are not inflating numbers. We are not hiding methodology. We are publicly committing to a higher standard than most petitions in Australia operate under.

OUR COMMITMENT

The Integrity Pledge

Fair Go Justice makes the following public commitments regarding petition numbers and verification:

  • We will never reset the supporter count to zero. 12,847 people engaged with this campaign. That is a fact. It stays.
  • We will never inflate numbers. Every supporter in our count took a deliberate action.
  • We will always publish our methodology on the Transparency page.
  • We will transition to identity-verified signatures before any formal parliamentary tabling.
  • We will not exclude rural and regional Australians by offering digital-only verification — Australia Post outlets provide the physical pathway.
  • All existing supporters will be offered the opportunity to upgrade their engagement to a formally verified signature — no one is removed, everyone can upgrade.

LEGISLATIVE BASIS

The verification infrastructure we are building upon is grounded in existing Australian law:

📚

Digital ID Act 2024 (Cth)

Establishes the Australian Government Digital ID System. myGov operates under this framework. Legally mandates privacy protections and prohibits misuse of identity data.

🔒

Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)

Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) apply to all identity data collected. We will not store identity data beyond what is required for verification confirmation.

🏭

Australia Post Digital ID

Australia Post's Digital Identity service is accredited under the Trusted Digital Identity Framework (TDIF). In-person verification available nationally.

🏛

JSCEM Petition Standards

The Joint Standing Committee on Electoral Matters sets standards for parliamentary petitions. Phase 4 targets full compliance with these standards.

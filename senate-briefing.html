Purpose of This Engagement

WHY FAIR GO JUSTICE IS IN PARLIAMENT

Australia is the only comparable common-law nation with no independent judicial integrity body. Our Legal Aid system is chronically underfunded. Evidence integrity protocols are non-existent. And AI is now making life-altering decisions in justice contexts — with zero mandatory oversight.

These aren't future risks. They are documented, measurable failures — backed by royal commission findings, court data, and academic research. Fair Go Justice brings these failures to Parliament with a specific, costed, politically viable solution: four targeted Acts that can be introduced in the 2026 legislative year.

The Legislative Package

FOUR ACTS. ONE MOVEMENT.

01
Proposed Legislation
Judicial Integrity Commission Act 2026
An independent, fully-funded body to investigate complaints against judicial officers at all levels. Modelled on ICAC — applied to the bench. 23+ comparable nations already have equivalent bodies.
02
Proposed Legislation
Legal Aid and Access to Justice Act 2026
Rebuild Australia's legal assistance infrastructure — doubled funding, modernised means tests, mandatory duty lawyers at every Magistrates' Court, and an AI-augmented online portal for self-represented litigants.
03
Proposed Legislation
Evidence Integrity and Digital Security Act 2026
Mandatory digital chain-of-custody protocols for all evidence in judicial proceedings. Criminalise evidence tampering with digital audit trails. Extend to AI-generated and AI-processed evidence.
04
NEW — Australia's First
AI Accountability in Justice Act 2026
Mandatory AI disclosure, human-in-the-loop requirements, and binding right to explanation for all automated decisions affecting individual rights. Aligns with PM's AI agenda — makes accountability binding, not voluntary.

Evidence Base

THE DATA BEHIND THE REFORMS

Every FGJ proposal is backed by primary sources — not opinion. Key evidence underpinning this engagement:

  • 73+ AI-fabricated citations submitted to Australian courts (UNSW Law, April 2026) — Federal Court issued formal warning
  • SRL rate tripled from 4.4% to 12.8% since 2019 (Federal Circuit Court Statistical Report 2024)
  • $548.5M Robodebt settlement — largest class action in Australian history (Royal Commission, 2023)
  • NLAP expired 30 June 2025 — 388,000 CLC turn-aways annually (National Association of Community Legal Centres)
  • Australia 26% / OECD 66% on judicial integrity criteria (OECD Justice at a Glance 2024)
  • Luck v Secretary, Services Australia [2025] FCAFC 26 — first Federal Court AI evidence case, February 2025
  • 18,000+ aged care recipients denied by algorithm without human review (AIHW 2025)

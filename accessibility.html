Accessibility

Last updated: September 2026

Fair Go Justice is committed to making this site accessible to everyone — including people with disability, older Australians, and people using assistive technology.

Our Commitment

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. This site is built with:

  • Semantic HTML headings and landmark regions
  • Sufficient colour contrast throughout
  • Keyboard-navigable menus and interactive elements
  • Alt text on images
  • Accessible labels on form fields and buttons
  • Content readable by screen readers without relying on decorative characters alone

Known Issues

We are a community movement, not a large organisation. Some pages may not yet meet every standard. We are actively working to improve accessibility as the site grows.

Report an Issue

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please contact us at fairgojustice@gmail.com. We aim to respond within five business days and will work to resolve issues as quickly as we can.

Alternative Formats

If you need content in an alternative format, please email us and we will do our best to assist.

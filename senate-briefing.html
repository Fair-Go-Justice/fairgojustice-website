Legal representation in Australia should not depend on your bank balance — and Advanced Intelligence systems entering our courts must be accountable, traceable and subject to human review.

Meeting: 22 September 2026
Five Pillars of Reform
Three Targeted Asks
Why This Meeting

SAME PROBLEM. DIFFERENT ROOM.

Senator Shoebridge has publicly raised the alarm about Advanced Intelligence being deployed by powerful institutions against working people — Atlassian (500 jobs), CommBank (300 jobs), Adore Beauty (40 jobs) — with no consultation, no worker protection, and no regulation in place to stop them.

Fair Go Justice documents the exact same dynamic playing out in a different room: Australian courts are now issuing mandatory Advanced Intelligence governance rules to self-represented litigants — ordinary people with no legal aid, no training, and no funded support to comply.

The institution is different: a court, not a corporation. The mechanism is different: a Practice Direction, not a restructuring notice. The pattern is identical: Advanced Intelligence deployed by the more powerful party against the less powerful party, with no regulation, no consultation, and no protection built in.

Fair Go Justice is on Senator Shoebridge's side of this argument. We are not asking him to embrace Advanced Intelligence in courts. We are asking him to champion the same principle he has already stated publicly: accountability, regulation, and human oversight before deployment — not after the damage is done.

Three Targeted Asks

WHAT WE'RE ASKING FOR

01

Senate Inquiry: Body-Worn Video and Advanced Intelligence in Courts

Mechanism: Legal & Constitutional Affairs References Committee motion
  • Police warn every citizen they are recording — that creates a reasonable expectation of access.
  • Footage is tendered by prosecution when it helps; withheld under GIPA when it doesn't.
  • Internal reviews are routinely rubber-stamped — not independently tested.
  • FCFCOA Practice Direction (29 May 2026): Family Court — 79% of litigants unrepresented — faces mandatory Advanced Intelligence governance with no funded support.
  • VLRC (3 Feb 2026): Australia's first law reform inquiry into Advanced Intelligence in courts — 30 recommendations — Senate has no equivalent federal inquiry.
  • Charlotin database (2 Jul 2026): 1,668 global Advanced Intelligence hallucination cases; 975 involve self-represented litigants — more than lawyers (653).
  • No parliamentary body has yet examined Advanced Intelligence specifically in courts.
02

Champion a National Judicial Integrity Commission

Mechanism: Parliamentary Joint Committee on the NACC; letter to the Attorney-General
  • Australia is the only comparable common-law nation without an independent federal judicial oversight body.
  • ALRC recommended a federal judicial commission in 2022 (Recommendation 5, Report 138).
  • Law Council has advocated for one since 2006.
  • Western Australia is actively legislating the model (WA Judicial Commission Bill 2026).
  • NACC Act excludes judicial officers by design — Operation Myrtleford (March 2026) exposed the grey zone.
  • The reasoning tree stops at the gavel — and that has to change.
03

Legal Medicare — Support FGJ's Five-Pillar Framework

Mechanism: Public endorsement; network introductions; Senate committee referrals
  • 380,000 Australians turned away from community legal centres annually.
  • 1.4M+ Australians with legal need unable to access the system.
  • Self-represented litigant rate tripled from 4.4% to 12.8%.
  • NLAP expired 30 June 2025 — no replacement.
  • $18.2B annual cost of unmet legal need.
  • $4.9B NPV from Advanced Intelligence integration; 1,050+ new funded positions; 22,000 sector-wide by 2030.
  • $2.25–$7 return per $1 invested.
Opening Hooks

SIX WAYS TO OPEN THE MEETING

01 — SAME PROBLEM, DIFFERENT ROOM
“You raised it about workers. We document it about court users. Advanced Intelligence deployed by a more powerful institution against someone with no resources and no protection — and zero regulation to stop it. The only difference is the room it happens in.”
02 — ROBODEBT PRECEDENT
“Robodebt settled for $548.5 million. Without binding Advanced Intelligence disclosure rules, the next one is already running.”
03 — THE AFFIDAVIT TRAP
“The Family Court now applies mandatory Advanced Intelligence governance to 79% unrepresented litigants — without funded support. Its 29 May 2026 Practice Direction says affidavit content must be ‘in the witness's own words.’ Legal Aid NSW is no longer accepting family law clients from 1 July 2026. The people who lost legal aid are told they cannot use the tool they have. That is a trap.”
04 — SELF-REPRESENTED LITIGANTS ARE THE MAJORITY
“The Charlotin database documented 1,668 Advanced Intelligence hallucination cases in court proceedings globally. 975 involve self-represented litigants — more than lawyers. This is not a risk. It is already happening.”
05 — VLRC: OFFICIAL RECOGNITION
“In February 2026 the Victorian Law Reform Commission published Australia's first law reform inquiry into Advanced Intelligence in courts. It recommends principles, guidelines, education and human oversight — not suppression. The Senate has not yet responded. Fair Go Justice's framework is that response.”
06 — BWV: USE IT OR LOSE IT
“Every time a citizen is pulled over, police say: this interaction is being recorded. That warning creates a reasonable expectation. The footage exists. But when citizens request it to clear their name, it is withheld. That is not transparency. That is a filter.”
